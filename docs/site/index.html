AI Ecommerce Workflow

Turn a product description, public URL, or image into a reviewable public-market brief before creating marketplace-specific drafts.

Open the published Skill (v1.1.1)

What it is

Research first. Adapt only after the marketplace is chosen.

It separates user-provided facts, image-visible details, public observations, and unknowns. The first result is a concise brief with comparable public links and observed display-price context. Copy, image requirements, and optional image briefs follow only after platform selection.

Workflow

A short sequence with visible review points

  1. Provide one inputProduct description, public product URL, or product image.
  2. Review public evidenceComparable links, source state, observed prices, selling points, and pain points.
  3. Select a marketplaceChoose the platform before platform-specific copy or image requirements are drafted.
  4. Check facts and claimsKeep missing specifications out of buyer-facing copy and return them as review items.
  5. Receive editable draftsUse the evidence ledger, copy pack, and image brief as an editable listing-preparation handoff.

Supported scope

Built for public-market research and accountable listing preparation

  • Public search evidence and selected-page Markdown handling
  • Marketplace adapters for Taobao/Tmall, JD, Pinduoduo, Douyin, Kuaishou, Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Shopify, and AliExpress
  • A complete ten-platform image-requirement matrix based on the designated public sources
  • Fact, category, claim, and marketplace-field review before delivery

Reviewable delivery

Evidence that stays with the draft

  • Source links, observed facts, and open review items travel with each research brief.
  • Platform fields, copy packs, image requirements, and optional image briefs are grouped for editing and handoff.
  • Fact, category, claim, and marketplace-field checks make each draft easier to review before use.
