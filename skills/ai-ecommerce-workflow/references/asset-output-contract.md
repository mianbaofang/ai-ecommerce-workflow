# Asset Output Contract

## Platform copy

Read `product-copy-framework.md` before writing. Build the internal fact ledger, select one category module, then apply the target marketplace adapter. After the final tone pass, read `listing-compliance-review.md` and rerun fact, claim, category, marketplace and prohibited-term review. Unverified attributes are omitted from buyer-facing copy and placed in the review checklist; do not make a storefront description sound like an internal QA report.

After the user selects a marketplace, return:

1. Three title variants within the platform's visible length rules.
2. Three to five benefit-led selling points.
3. A product description ordered by the category's buyer questions.
4. Five FAQ entries grounded in available product information.
5. A keyword and prohibited-claim review.

Unsupported claims are marked `待确认` in the internal review checklist and are not silently rewritten as facts. Ask only when a missing category field blocks safe purchase guidance or platform compliance.

## Images

Ask for the model/tool, purpose, reference-image rights, ratio, size, count, style, text policy, and delivery format before generation.

Prefer a real product reference and derive platform variants through crop, background, expansion, or layout adaptation. Preserve product geometry, color, logo, packaging text, and material appearance. Do not generate a different product for each marketplace.

Build the visual sequence around buyer decisions:

1. The first product image or cover identifies the product and handles one primary decision only; do not stack every selling point into it.
2. Later images answer distinct buyer questions with evidence: scale or dimensions, SKU differences, structure or detail, fit or use, package contents, limitations, care, or service terms.
3. Order detail-page sections as recognition and context, fact-backed solution, specifications or compatibility, variants and package contents, then limitations and purchase concerns.
4. Follow the selected platform's permitted image count and surfaces. The former fixed five-image / five-screen layout is a planning pattern, not a universal quota.
5. Record each asset's purpose, supporting fact, platform surface, and review status so decorative images do not replace missing product evidence.

Return a complete image-requirement matrix for the selected platforms, a shot list, prompt or generation record, and a manual review checklist. Generated media is a draft or creative reference until the user confirms product fidelity and marketplace compliance.

Read `platform-image-specs.md` before preparing the matrix. Its ten-platform image requirements come from the two user-designated references. For every asset, record the platform, surface, pixel dimensions, aspect ratio, format, byte size, observation date, and current-rule review status. Do not fill a missing platform value by copying another platform's size.
